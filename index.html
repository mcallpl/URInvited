Publishing a new one
Step 1

Open New event and fill in the form. The preview updates as you type.

Step 2

Download the two files and drop them into a folder named after the event, at the top level of the site.

Step 3

Paste the printed line into assets/events-index.js so it appears here, then deploy.

The folder name is the public URL. PhilMcAllister/ is served at /PhilMcAllister/. Avoid the reserved names assets, images, public and deploy.
